# Answers (Questions 8, 9, 10)

## Question 8: Electricity vs Electronics
**Electricity** is the study and use of electric power, mainly involving the generation, transmission, and consumption of electrical energy.

**Electronics** focuses on controlling electric current using components such as diodes, transistors, and integrated circuits.

### Key Differences
- Electricity → Power and energy delivery  
- Electronics → Signal control and processing  

---

## Question 9: Example Device – Smartphone
A smartphone depends heavily on electronics.

### Role of Components
- **Transistors:** Processor and logic circuits  
- **Capacitors:** Store energy and filter noise  
- **Integrated Circuits:** Communication and control  
- **Sensors:** Convert physical inputs into electrical signals  

Electronics enables calling, internet use, cameras, and apps.

---

## Question 10: Scope of Electricity and Electronics

### Electricity Applications
- Power generation and distribution  
- Wiring, motors, lighting  

### Electronics Applications
- Communication systems  
- Medical devices  
- Consumer gadgets  

Both fields are essential in modern engineering.
